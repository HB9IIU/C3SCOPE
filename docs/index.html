Flash · Download · Play

A 5 EUR QO-100 spectrum viewer. Two minutes from a blank Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3 to a live waterfall. Other ESP32 boards can be flashed too.

Step 1 · do this here, in your browser

Flash your ESP32

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari and phones cannot flash over serial.
  • Plug your board into USB with a data cable. Close any serial monitor.
  • If connecting fails, hold the B (boot) button while plugging the board in.

AConnect your board

Click Connect and pick USB Serial Device (or USB JTAG/serial) in the browser's list. The page recognises the chip and selects the matching firmware for you.

Board
Not connected

BInstall the receiver firmware

Build date

Installing erases everything on the board and replaces it with the receiver firmware. It takes about a minute. Do not unplug the board.

The Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3 is the board C3SCOPE is made and tested for. Other ESP32 chips are offered too (ESP32, C2, C5, C6, C61, H2, S2, S3, S31); they run the same ESP-SDR receiver, but have not been tested with C3SCOPE's QO-100 settings.

Loading firmware catalog…

When it says the firmware is installed, you are done with step 1. Unplug and replug the board.

Step 3 · the fun part

Play

Plug in the flashed XIAO and double-click C3SCOPE.bat. Your browser opens, C3SCOPE finds the board by itself, and the live QO-100 spectrum and waterfall (2401–2410 MHz) appear.

Levels are relative, not calibrated. C3SCOPE only listens: never connect a transmitter to the board.

Connection problems
  • The board is not listed: check the cable carries data, close other serial programs, and try another USB socket.
  • Windows can give the same COM number to your board and to a virtual serial port from other radio software (for example FlexRadio, com0com or VSPE). If connecting fails, open Device Manager, Ports (COM & LPT), double-click the board's USB Serial Device, Port Settings, Advanced, pick another COM port number, then unplug and replug the board.
  • Still stuck: hold B while plugging the board in, then connect again.
