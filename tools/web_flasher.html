C3SCOPE · Step 1: flash your ESP32-C3

Turn a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3 (about 5 EUR) into a C3SCOPE receiver, right here in your browser. Nothing to install. Then download the Windows app (step 2) and play (step 3).

Before you start

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari and most phones cannot flash over serial.
  2. Plug the XIAO ESP32-C3 into a USB port with a data cable. Close any serial monitor.
  3. If connecting fails, hold the B (boot) button while plugging the board in, then try again.

Installing erases everything on the board and replaces it with the C3SCOPE receiver firmware.

Connection problems
  • The board is not listed: check the cable carries data, unplug other serial programs, and try another USB socket.
  • Windows can give the same COM number to your board and to a virtual serial port from other radio software (for example FlexRadio, com0com or VSPE). If connecting fails, open Device Manager, Ports (COM & LPT), double-click the board's USB Serial Device, Port Settings, Advanced, pick another COM port number, then unplug and replug the board.
  • Still stuck: hold B while plugging the board in, then connect again.
